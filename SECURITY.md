# Security Policy

We take the security of reelcrew seriously: the CLI, the video skills, the update channel,
and the account and licensing service.

## Reporting a vulnerability

**Please do not report security vulnerabilities through public GitHub issues or discussions.**

Report them privately:

1. **GitHub private vulnerability reporting (preferred)**: use the
   [**Report a vulnerability**](https://github.com/agentvid/reelcrew-support/security/advisories/new)
   button under this repository's **Security** tab. It opens a private advisory visible only
   to you and the maintainers.
2. **Email**: if you cannot use GitHub, contact the maintainers privately through the
   address on the reelcrew website.

## What to include

- The affected part (CLI command, skill, installer, update, account service) and the version
  from `reelcrew --version`.
- A description of the vulnerability and its impact.
- Steps to reproduce or a proof of concept.
- A suggested mitigation, if you have one.

Redact secrets, tokens, license keys, credentials and personal data from anything you share.

## Our commitment

- We acknowledge your report as soon as possible.
- We keep you informed while we investigate and fix.
- We credit you for the discovery when a fix is released, unless you prefer to stay anonymous.

Thank you for helping keep reelcrew and its users safe.
